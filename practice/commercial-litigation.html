Commercial Litigation

Practice Area · TEO JENG YU & PARTNERS

Commercial disputes rarely stay confined to a single issue. A contract dispute can quickly raise questions of urgent relief, related corporate disputes or exposure to a counterparty's insolvency, and the strategy needs to account for all of it from the outset. We act for corporations, financial institutions and business owners in contractual disputes, shareholder conflicts, fraud-related claims and complex civil litigation, from the initial letter of demand through to trial and appeal. Where a matter is urgent, we are able to move quickly to apply for interim relief, including injunctions, while the broader dispute is worked through.

  • Contract disputes
  • Urgent relief and injunctions
  • Corporate disputes
  • Trial & appellate advocacy
