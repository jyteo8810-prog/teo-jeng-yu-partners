Debt & Asset Recovery

Practice Area · TEO JENG YU & PARTNERS

For a creditor, a favourable judgment is a means to an end, not the end itself. Recovery strategy should be built around what is actually achievable given the debtor's assets and financial position. We advise financial institutions and corporate creditors on recovery strategy from the initial demand through negotiation, proceedings, judgment and enforcement, including security enforcement and insolvency-related recovery. Where a debtor's position is uncertain, we help clients weigh the cost of further proceedings against the realistic prospects of recovery.

  • Recovery strategy
  • Security enforcement
  • Judgment enforcement
  • Insolvency strategy
