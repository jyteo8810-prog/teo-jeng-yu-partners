PRACTICE AREA · 02

Construction & Infrastructure

Practice Area · TEO JENG YU & PARTNERS

Construction disputes sit at the intersection of contract, engineering fact and commercial pressure. A payment dispute or defects claim can affect project cash flow and completion timelines well before it reaches a tribunal. We advise developers, employers, main contractors, subcontractors, consultants and suppliers on the full range of construction issues, including payment claims, variations, delay and disruption, defects and CIPAA adjudication. Because construction disputes often need to be resolved without stopping the project, we work closely with our clients' project teams to keep legal strategy aligned with what is happening on site.

  • Payment disputes
  • Variations & delay
  • Defects
  • CIPAA / adjudication
