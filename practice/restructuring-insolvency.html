Restructuring & Insolvency

Practice Area · TEO JENG YU & PARTNERS

Financial distress raises different questions depending on which side of the table you sit on. A company may be looking to restructure and preserve the business; a creditor may be assessing the best route to recovery. We advise companies, directors, creditors and financial institutions on restructuring options, judicial management, schemes of arrangement, liquidation and the practical consequences of each for stakeholders. Timing matters. We work with clients to assess their position early, while the widest range of options is still realistically available.

  • Restructuring
  • Judicial management
  • Liquidation
  • Creditor recovery
