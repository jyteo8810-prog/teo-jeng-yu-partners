Debt Recovery Strategy: Why Enforcement Planning Matters

Legal Insight · TEO JENG YU & PARTNERS

Debt recovery is not merely about obtaining a judgment. For a financial institution or corporate creditor, the commercial objective is usually recovery of value within a realistic timeframe. An early recovery assessment should consider the debt documentation, security, guarantees, debtor assets, corporate structure, competing creditors and insolvency risk. A recovery strategy may involve demand, negotiation, restructuring, proceedings and enforcement. The appropriate route depends on the evidence, amount at stake, available security and the debtor's financial position. Creditors should also think beyond judgment. The practical question is what assets or enforcement mechanisms are realistically available after judgment and whether insolvency proceedings may affect recovery. Our practice includes strategic debt and asset recovery solutions for financial institutions and corporate clients through negotiation, litigation and enforcement.
