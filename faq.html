07 / FAQ

Questions businesses often ask.

What should a company do after receiving a letter of demand?

Preserve relevant documents, identify the contractual position, check deadlines and seek an early assessment before making substantive admissions.

When should a developer or contractor consider CIPAA?

Where a qualifying construction contract gives rise to a payment dispute, CIPAA may provide a statutory adjudication route. The contract and facts should be reviewed first.

Can your firm assist financial institutions with recovery?

Our practice includes debt and asset recovery, enforcement, insolvency and commercial disputes. The precise scope should be discussed based on the institution's requirements.

Can you assist employers with domestic inquiries?

Yes. Employment practice includes disciplinary proceedings, domestic inquiries, misconduct, termination, retrenchment and related disputes.
